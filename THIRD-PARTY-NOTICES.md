# Third-party notices

Bytos is built on the Apache License 2.0 and bundles open-source dependencies.
This page is the human-readable entry point. The authoritative, machine-generated
inventory that ships inside the VSIX is:

- [`apps/vscode/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`](apps/vscode/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt) —
  2,396 packages with their declared license and copyright line.
- [`apps/vscode/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.json`](apps/vscode/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.json) —
  the same inventory as structured data, including the resolution status of every
  entry.

The inventory is regenerated by
`apps/vscode/scripts/generate-third-party-notices.mjs` during packaging and is
validated by `apps/vscode/scripts/check-licenses.mjs`. Do not hand-edit the
generated files.

The 13 unresolved entries are all covered by documented exceptions in
`apps/vscode/scripts/license-exceptions.json`: they are proprietary packages that
are not part of the shipped production bundle, or development/packaging tools.
No GPL or AGPL package is present in the inventory. Copyleft-adjacent
dual-license entries are exercised through their permissive option (e.g.
`jszip` under MIT, `node-forge` under BSD-3-Clause); the single LGPL entry
(`jschardet`) is not imported or bundled anywhere.
